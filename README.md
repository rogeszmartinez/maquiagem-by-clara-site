# Clara Galvão

Create a premium, elegant and highly polished one-page landing website for **Maria Clara Galvão**, a professional makeup artist based in Lauro de Freitas, Bahia, Brazil.

The entire website copy must be written in **Brazilian Portuguese**, even though these instructions are written in English.

## PROJECT GOAL

The website should establish Maria Clara as a professional, trustworthy and premium makeup artist, while feeling welcoming and personal.

The main objectives are:

* increase credibility and professionalism
* centralize her portfolio and services
* make it easy for potential clients to contact and book her
* improve her presence in Google searches
* clearly communicate her service options, prices and booking policies
* differentiate her from local competitors through a sophisticated visual identity

Her desired perception is:
**acolhedora, profissional e sofisticada/premium.**

She describes her work as:
**beleza, qualidade e excelência.**

She has been working professionally in the area for approximately **5 years**.

Main Instagram:
**@maquiagembyclara**

## VISUAL DIRECTION

Use the provided reference website as inspiration for the overall structure and simplicity:
https://www.mariasilveirapsi.com.br/

Do NOT copy the reference website's branding or content. Use its clean editorial structure only as inspiration.

The visual style should feel:

* sophisticated
* feminine without being cliché
* luxurious
* editorial
* minimal
* warm
* refined
* welcoming
* professional

Avoid:

* generic beauty salon aesthetics
* excessive pink
* overly colorful gradients
* cheap-looking gold effects
* excessive rounded cards
* excessive animations
* stock-photo aesthetics
* AI-generated-looking imagery
* visually crowded layouts

The design should feel closer to a **high-end beauty editorial / luxury personal brand** than a generic salon website.

### Suggested visual system

Use an elegant neutral palette:

* warm ivory / off-white background
* soft beige / champagne secondary tones
* dark espresso / near-black typography
* subtle muted gold accents only where appropriate

Typography should combine:

* an elegant high-contrast serif for major headings
* a clean modern sans-serif for body text, buttons and navigation

Use generous whitespace, strong typography hierarchy, editorial composition and carefully controlled image placement.

The website should feel premium even when viewed on a mobile phone.

* O valor restante é pago no dia do evento.

* O horário somente será reservado mediante envio do comprovante de pagamento.
* A disponibilidade deve ser verificada antes do depósito.
